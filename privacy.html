Privacy Policy

Yoteii · Last updated: November 3, 2026

Yoteii is a daily schedule app that builds your day from your wake time. This policy explains what information the app handles and what it does with it. In short: your data stays on your device, and the only time it goes anywhere else is if you choose to back it up to your own Google Drive.

What Yoteii stores

Everything you enter, such as your schedule, tasks, to-dos, templates, history and settings, is saved on your own device (in the browser's or app's local storage). The developer does not run a server for Yoteii and cannot see, read or access this data.

What Yoteii does not do

  • No advertising and no ad trackers.
  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your information.
  • No account is required. You can use Yoteii with “Continue without an account”.
  • The app's fonts are bundled, so it does not load fonts from outside services.

Optional: Sign in with Google and Drive backup

If you choose “Continue with Google”, Yoteii asks for these permissions:

  • Your email address and basic profile (name): used only to show which Google account is signed in.
  • Google Drive “app data” access (drive.appdata): lets Yoteii save and restore a single backup file in a private, hidden folder in your Google Drive. Yoteii can only access its own folder. It cannot see or change any of your other Drive files, and the backup does not appear in your normal Drive file list.

The backup is sent directly between your device and Google. It does not pass through any server run by the developer. Backups are made only while you are signed in, either when you press “Back up now” or if you turn on automatic backup.

Yoteii’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The information is used only to provide the sign-in and backup features described above. It is not used for advertising, not transferred to others, and not read by people.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Sign out in Settings → Google Drive to stop backups at any time.
  • Delete data on your device using the delete option in the app’s Settings, or by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it.
  • Delete your Drive backup or remove access in your Google Account under Security → third-party apps and services (or Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps), where you can disconnect Yoteii and delete its hidden app data.

Children

Yoteii is a general-purpose planner and is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
